Frequently Asked Questions

How do I flip the coin?

Tap the coin, tap the Flip button, or shake your phone. It lands on a fair, random Heads or Tails every time.

How do I switch to the dice?

Tap Coin, 1 Die or 2 Dice at the top of the screen, or swipe left or right on the background (outside the coin). You can switch any time.

Is the flip really random?

Yes. Every flip and roll uses the system's secure random generator, giving a genuine 50/50 (or 1‑in‑6) outcome. Results are never influenced by your streak.

How do I change the coin color or background?

Open Settings (the gear icon). You can switch the coin between gold and silver, change the background to dark, felt green or black, set the dice to white or black, and even pick any emoji for each coin face.

How do I remove ads?

In Settings → Remove Ads, you can make a one‑time purchase that removes all ads forever. If you reinstall or switch devices, tap Restore Purchases to get it back at no cost.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads.

Open Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase. If it still doesn't work, email us and we'll sort it out.

Do you collect my data?

No accounts, no sign‑in, and your stats stay on your device. The app sends anonymous usage statistics (never linked to you, never used for tracking) to help us improve it — turn them off any time in Settings → Privacy → Share Usage Data. See our Privacy Policy for full details, including advertising.
